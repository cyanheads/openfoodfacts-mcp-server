# 0.3.5 — 2026-09-22

## Added

- **`off_browse_taxonomy` ranks the exact-term tag first** — among live suggestions, the tag spelling the search term itself (`en:lentils` for "lentil") now sorts ahead of compounds instead of being cut by a small `limit`; the live pool is requested in full to make the ranking possible (#42).

## Fixed

- **The HTTP status decides retryability, not the body** — a 501 is now rejected once as `upstream_rejected` instead of retried four times, and an HTML-bodied 4xx is described as a refusal rather than blamed on load shedding (#37).
- **`off_get_product` parsed ingredients keep their sub-ingredients** — nested entries (wheat flour under "cereal", milk under cheddar) are now carried under their parent up to three levels deep instead of being dropped (#40).
- **Output descriptions corrected** — `off_get_product`'s `barcode` now describes the echoed input rather than "returned by the API"; the Nutri-Score and Green-Score descriptions name their full grade vocabularies (`unknown`, `not-applicable`, `a-plus`); `off_browse_taxonomy` no longer advertises a `tags[].products` count no backend ever supplies (#43).
