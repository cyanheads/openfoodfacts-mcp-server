# 0.4.3 — 2026-11-02

## Changed

- **`mirror_as_of` description** ([#81](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfoodfacts-mcp-server/issues/81)): `off_get_product`, `off_search_products`, and `off_compare_products` describe it as the time up to which the local copy reflects every product contributed or edited on Open Food Facts. The README and `docs/design.md` match.

## Fixed

- **`mirror_as_of` during a full reload** ([#81](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfoodfacts-mcp-server/issues/81)): the load now lowers the cited as-of to the older of the one it began with and the newest `last_modified_t` among the rows it has stamped, ahead of the rows it rewrites, so an answer never cites a time newer than its data. A run that resumes a load a 0.4.2 build stopped makes that move before it reads on, and a mirror read cites the older of the as-of read before and after the data.
- **Full load that stamps no product** ([#82](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfoodfacts-mcp-server/issues/82)): a load that stamped no product across every run it took fails before its sweep with `ServiceUnavailable` reason `export_no_products`, where it had emptied the store and recorded an as-of of 1970-01-01. The store and its checkpoint stay as they were, the download is deleted, and the next refresh downloads the export again. `mirror:init`, `mirror:refresh`, and the scheduled job exit 1 through their existing error paths.

## Dependencies

- **`tsc-alias`** ^1.9.9 → ^1.9.10 (the `devcheck.config.json` `outdated.allowlist` hold is removed).
