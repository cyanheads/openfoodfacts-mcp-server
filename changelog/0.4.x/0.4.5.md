# 0.4.5 — 2026-11-24

## Added

- **`lang`, `ingredients_text_languages`, `image_ingredients_url`, `image_nutrition_url`** ([#91](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfoodfacts-mcp-server/issues/91)): `off_get_product` names the record's main language, returns the ingredients text in every language entered (empty entries left out), and links the ingredients and nutrition photos to check it against. All four are `fields` values, and `ingredients_text` and `ingredients_text_languages` each bring `lang`.
- **`nutriscore_score`, `ecoscore_score`** ([#93](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfoodfacts-mcp-server/issues/93)): the points behind each grade, on `off_compare_products` rows and `off_get_product`, printed beside the grade (`b (1)`). Nutri-Score points rank products of one kind only. In an `off_get_product` `fields` subset each brings its grade.
- **`quantity`, `completeness`** ([#92](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfoodfacts-mcp-server/issues/92)): on every `off_search_products` row.
- **`last_modified`, `created`** ([#96](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfoodfacts-mcp-server/issues/96)): ISO 8601 record times on `off_search_products` rows and `off_get_product`, and `fields` values there. A text-search row carries the indexed revision's.
- **`text_index_as_of`** ([#95](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfoodfacts-mcp-server/issues/95)): the time of the newest record in the text index, on `off_search_products` answers the index served. `text_index_snapshot` names it; with no date it keeps its undated wording.
- **`mirror_fields_unread` on `off_compare_products` rows**: a row served from a mirror row that predates the score points names the fields it lacks. The comparison sends no live read for them; `off_get_product` reads them.

## Changed

- **Mirror record version 3**: a mirror-backed server fully reloads its store on its next refresh. Until the reload reaches a product, a full `off_get_product` read of it goes live for the fields the older row lacks, while `off_compare_products` stays on the mirror and names them in `mirror_fields_unread`. Stored products grow about 10% (mean 4,387 → 4,829 bytes in the fixture sample).
- **Text index date probe**: a text search beside its own request reads the index date, at most one probe per 6 hours (per 15 minutes while it keeps failing), drawing on the `OFF_RATE_LIMIT_SEARCH` budget only while two slots are free.
- **`brands_tag` on the mirror** ([#94](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfoodfacts-mcp-server/issues/94)): a tag-only search is served from the mirror only for a brand it holds products under; any other brand reads live. For such a brand, `page` 11 or later sends one live count: matches that end before the page answer it empty with that total, and matches that reach into it fail as `page_out_of_range` naming the brand and the live total.

## Fixed

- **Total carbohydrates** ([#90](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfoodfacts-mcp-server/issues/90)): a record holding only `carbohydrates-total` no longer reads as Carbohydrates `N/A`. `carbohydrates_total_100g` is its own `off_get_product` nutriment and `off_compare_products` column, never derived from `carbohydrates_100g`, and Sugars and Saturated fat no longer nest under a parent line the record lacks.
- **`brands_tag`** ([#94](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfoodfacts-mcp-server/issues/94)): a brand name is slugged as Open Food Facts slugs it (`Nestlé` → `nestle`, `Meßmer` → `messmer`), where an accent returned an exact zero; brand synonyms resolve only on a search reading the live database.
- **Mirror serving size**: a household measure with a mass in brackets (`1/2 cup (60 g)`) reads back in the mass's unit, as a live read does.
- **Record times**: `last_modified` and `created` are omitted for a time at or before the epoch, never reported as one.
- **`completeness`**: a numeric string Open Food Facts stored (`"0.65"`) is read as the number.
- **Singular totals**: a total of 1 reads "1 total product" and "1 matching product exists".
- **Empty-result line**: it no longer points every search at `off_browse_taxonomy`; a brand-only search is pointed at `query`, since the taxonomy holds no brands.
- **Page 11 or later refusal**: an unconfirmed value's recovery hint goes by why it went unconfirmed: a value no tag matches is to be corrected or dropped, and one whose lookup failed is to be retried.
- **Brands and taxonomy values ending in Σ**: a text search sends an unconfirmed one under both IDs as one OR group, so `categories_tag: "EL:ΧΥΜΌΣ"` with a nutrient filter finds the 2 products filed under `el:χυμός`. Such a value and its `σ`-typed twin no longer share one cached taxonomy lookup.
